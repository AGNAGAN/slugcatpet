This little program doesn't come with the game's character assets itself; it reads the images once from the Rain World installed on your computer.
You need:
  · A legitimate copy of "Rain World" on Steam, installed on this computer
  · Its DLC "Downpour" (the importer reads its sprite sheets too)

Then:
1. Unzip first. Right-click the archive, choose "Extract All", and extract it somewhere easy to find, like the desktop.
2. Go into the extracted folder, find LizardPet.exe, and double-click it.
3. The first launch takes a few seconds to a minute while it reads the assets from the game.

How to use: click the arrow on the right edge of the screen to open the sidebar. Place poles, an egg bug and lizards on the desktop; drag them with the mouse and throw them. There are six kinds (green, pink, blue, cyan, white, red), up to 3 at once: pink lizards climb poles, green lizards stay on the ground, and white lizards camouflage until nearly invisible when still. Lizards hunt egg bugs, carry them off through the screen edge to eat, and come back after a while.
Tray icon: click it to show or hide the sidebar; the right-click menu clears the field, switches language, toggles launch at startup, and quits.
What is on the field is saved on exit and restored on the next launch (~/.slugcatpet/lizardpet.json). It can run alongside Slugcat Pet.

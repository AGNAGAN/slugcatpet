This little program doesn't come with the game's character assets itself; it needs to read the picture once from the Rain World already installed on your computer.
So you need to meet these first:
  · Own a legitimate copy of "Rain World" on Steam, already installed on this computer
  · Also have its DLC "Downpour" to have more slugcats.

Once the prerequisites are met:
1. Unzip first. Right-click the archive, choose "Extract All", and extract it somewhere easy to find, like the desktop.
2. Go into the extracted folder, find SlugcatPet.exe, and double-click it.
3. The first launch takes a few to a few dozen seconds to initialize, fetching the character assets from the game.

The interface follows your system language. Switch it in Settings or the tray menu.

To change skins or colors: "Appearance" next to a cat in Settings. Put skins from others in the skins folder (Settings → Open skins folder); its README.txt explains the rest.
